I'm a senior AI engineer at Enocta and the founder of [Eschatia Labs](https://eschatialabs.com), an independent, non-profit open-source AI research lab. I build small models that run on a laptop, agents you can inspect and detectors that show their evidence.

## Career

- **Senior AI Engineer**, Enocta, since 2025. Assistants that answer from company documents (RAG), an agentic customer-success workflow, role-play training simulations.
- **Full-stack and Mobile Engineer**, Credit Bureau of Turkey (KKB), 2023-2024. Financial software used across the Turkish banking sector: backend, mobile and system design.
- **AI and Full-stack Engineer**, AgriDL5.0, 2022-2023 (contract). Models that predict growing conditions in vertical farms, plus the Flutter app on top.
- **Backend and Data Engineer**, Alictus, 2021-2022. Moved internal tools to the cloud with Go and Azure.
- **Internships** at SimBT, BiSoft and Roar Games Studio, 2019-2021. Defence technology software, an NLTK and Neo4j chatbot before LLMs, Unity and C# games.

## Projects

- **[Emberwick](https://emberwick.mertkayacs.com)**: A medieval village you play in the browser. Three small decision models decide what the villagers do through storms, fires, markets and wolves.
- **[reevesagents](https://github.com/mertkayacs/reevesagents)**: Run Claude Code, Codex and 20+ other coding assistants side by side in one terminal, with one of them coordinating the rest. CLI, TUI, Web UI and MCP server. 107 stars, 2,375 npm downloads a month.
- **[JevAlt](https://github.com/mertkayacs/jevalt)**: The three 4B decision models behind Emberwick, for English, Turkish and German. Calibrated answers on a laptop with about 3 GB of memory. 3,505 Hugging Face model downloads.
- **[xdfdet](https://github.com/mertkayacs/xdfdet)**: Explainable deepfake detection: eight EfficientNet-B4 models with Grad-CAM evidence, the code behind my thesis and conference paper. 1,133 Hugging Face downloads.
- **[Tholos](https://github.com/mertkayacs/tholos)**: Small local assistants that share tables, notes and a task board, every action behind a rule. Its model, Tholos-2B: 2,248 Hugging Face downloads.
- **[Ultra Mod](https://github.com/mertkayacs/ultramod)**: The all-in-one mod pack for Claude Code. 2,224 npm downloads a month.

Download counts from 25 October 2026: 10,341 on Hugging Face (6,886 for my models, 3,455 for my datasets) and 10,018 for my npm packages.
